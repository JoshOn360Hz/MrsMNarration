Meet Clare

A voice listeners can settle into.

I’m Clare, a UK-based narrator with a love for language, layered characters and the quiet magic of a story told aloud.

From intimate memoir to sweeping fiction, I bring a thoughtful, collaborative approach to every project. My delivery is warm and assured, with an ear for natural rhythm and the detail that makes a world feel real.

Whether you’re an independent author, publisher or creative studio, you can expect considered communication, consistent audio and a performance that serves the writing.
